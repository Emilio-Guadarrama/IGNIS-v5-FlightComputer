## 0. The five things to read first

1. **Pyro: one failed part can fire a channel, and the MOSFET gates have no resistor to ground.** There is no arming element independent of the fire path, and the Q2/Q5 gates are held off only by a PNP follower at about one Vbe. Findings AR-01, PY-01. **Sign-off required.**
2. **The firmware reads battery voltage 6.3 % high.** `VBAT_DIVIDER_RATIO` is 0.3007 in `pins_v5.h`; the netlist divider is 47k/147k = 0.3197. Finding BT-01, deferred item D-03. **Sign-off required.**
3. **U1 (AMS1117-5.0) cannot carry the simultaneous-peak load.** At 8.4 V in it dissipates about 2.1 W for a 0.62 A load, and its tab pad has no net, so it cannot be tied to thermal copper as drawn. Findings PW-01, PW-03.
4. **`PCB_IGNIS-v5.PcbDoc` is behind `Main.NET`.** It has no routing, a 2-layer stack, 87 of 107 components, and its Storage nets are from before the 10 November schematic change. Finding PCB-01.
5. **SD failures are no longer silent.** Phase 1 and 2 are done, host tests 58/58, firmware compiles. Not tested on hardware.
